ABOUT SHEGA

Heritage, Presented Forward

Shega was founded on a simple belief: Ethiopian traditional clothing deserves a place in modern life, not just in the archives. Every piece we make begins with the techniques our grandmothers used at the loom—the same tilet patterns, the same handspun cotton—and is finished with a cut and fit built for how people actually dress today.

We work directly with weavers across Ethiopia, paying fairly for skill that takes years to master. The result is clothing that carries real cultural weight, made to be worn with pride at a wedding, a holiday, or a Tuesday that deserves a little more elegance.
